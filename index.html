AI-Based Crop Disease Identification

Project #9

Project Purpose

Developed as part of the academic requirements for Project #9, this platform provides an image classification prototype designed to identify selected crop diseases from leaf images. The system is built to support Malawi's agricultural sector by empowering farmers, extension workers, and agricultural officers to detect plant health issues early and apply timely localized treatments.

How the AI Identification Process Works

1. Select & Upload

The user selects the target crop and uploads a clear leaf image from the field for diagnosis.

2. Image Classification

The AI model studies leaf color, spot patterns, and disease symptoms against a labeled dataset.

3. Results & Treatment

The system provides the likely disease, confidence level, severity estimate, and recommended response.

Why This Matters for Agriculture

Early Detection

Spotting disease early helps reduce crop loss and prevents the spread of infection across nearby plants.

Better Farm Decisions

Farmers and agricultural officers can act faster using visible symptoms, disease risk alerts, and guidance.

Cost Savings

Timely action reduces unnecessary spraying, protects yields, and helps farmers manage input costs wisely.

Food Security

Healthy crops support stable production, income generation, and food availability in rural communities.

What the System Supports

  • ✔️ Crop disease recognition from uploaded leaf images
  • ✔️ Support for major crops such as maize, tobacco, tomato, tea, and cassava
  • ✔️ Simple interface for farmers, extension workers, and agricultural officers
  • ✔️ Fast decision support for field diagnosis and treatment planning
